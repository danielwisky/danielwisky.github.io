{%- comment -%} Resolves the cover URL of a post. Usage: include with post=post (or post=page, on the post page). Variants: cover (default) post header, and the card on retina screens; photos come out 1200x800 (3:2), tag covers are 4:3 SVG card 560x420, for the feed card (via srcset); for tag covers it's the same SVG as the cover thumb smaller, for the 72px in the sidebar and search og 1200x630 bitmap for og:image, since social networks don't accept SVG If the requested variant isn't in the map, falls back to the cover. Precedence: 1. cover-img in the front matter, to point to a specific or external image 2. own photo at _photos/.jpg, optimized by scripts/build-photos.mjs 3. cover generated from the first tag, by scripts/build-covers.mjs 4. default cover Prints only the URL, with no surrounding whitespace, so it can go straight into an attribute. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign p = include.post -%} {%- assign variant = include.variant | default: 'cover' -%} {%- assign photo = site.data.photos[p.slug] -%} {%- assign tag_cover = site.data.covers[p.tags.first] -%} {%- if p['cover-img'] -%} {{- p['cover-img'] | relative_url -}} {%- elsif photo -%} {{- photo[variant] | default: photo.cover | relative_url -}} {%- elsif tag_cover -%} {{- tag_cover[variant] | default: tag_cover.cover | relative_url -}} {%- elsif variant == 'thumb' -%} {{- '/assets/img/covers/thumbs/default.svg' | relative_url -}} {%- elsif variant == 'og' -%} {{- '/assets/img/covers/og/default.jpg' | relative_url -}} {%- else -%} {{- '/assets/img/covers/default.svg' | relative_url -}} {%- endif -%}
